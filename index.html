Hear Me Out

IXD 415 presentation schedule, Fall 2026

What this is

Every week one person gives a short presentaton to the class. The theme is hear me out: a conspiracy theory you believe (or could plausibly believe), or a boring-seeming topic that is actually fascinating. Convince us to think differently than we did ten minutes ago.

One person presents per week, on a rolling basis. The book — Everything I Know about Life I Learned from PowerPoint by Russell Davies — passes to the next person on the list the week before they present.
